## Production Config Observed In Repo

`wrangler.jsonc` includes public Supabase URL/publishable key and native alert setup/registration enabled, with native delivery disabled. It pins seller checkout and all ad-credit purchase gates to `false`, fixes the Apple/Google application identities, and keeps Apple sandbox and Google test purchases `false`. It does not include Apple certificate material, Google service-account credentials, Pub/Sub account/subscription identifiers, Stripe secrets, seller URLs, seller account identifiers, Firebase private key, service-role, or mail password values.

## Secret Handling Rules

- Do not print or commit secret values.
- Do not copy production secrets into preview or docs.
- Keep native signing files and provider config files out of Git.
- The only current seller-link rollback control is `TTC_SELLER_CHECKOUT_LINKS_ENABLED=false`. Keep legacy official/marketplace Merch and destination-routing switches false; never use them as future seller-payout controls. The checkout master, booking, and booking-provider onboarding gates are separate current controls and also remain false until their approved rollout.
- Keep `TTC_SELLER_CHECKOUT_LINKS_ENABLED=false` until the protected migration, deployment approval, one privately reviewed seller-supplied live link, web/Android/TestFlight iPad QA, legal/privacy review, and rollback proof are complete.
- Keep `TTC_NATIVE_PUSH_DELIVERY_ENABLED=false` until Android and iOS delivery evidence is complete.
- Keep `TTC_CSP_ENFORCE_ENABLED` unset or `false` until production Report-Only evidence is reviewed.
